"About Us"

Fashion designers create original clothing, footwear, and accessories by blending artistic vision with market research and technical production skills.

Famous design companies

fendi

Fendi is a famous Italian Fendi luxury fashion house that creates high-end fur, ready-to-wear clothing, leather goods, shoes, and accessories.

louis

Louis Vuitton is the world's largest and most valuable luxury brand, famous for its high-end leather goods, trunks, and the iconic LV monogram.

gucci

Guccio Gucci worked as a porter at the Savoy Hotel in London, where he admired the luxury luggage of wealthy guests. Inspired, he returned to Florence and opened a shop selling fine leather goods and equestrian gear. During wartime material shortages in the 1930s and 1940s, the brand innovated by using alternative materials like woven hemp and heated bamboo, which created the famous Bamboo Bag.
